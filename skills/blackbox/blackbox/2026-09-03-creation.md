e.g. the owner makes a typo, the assistant rewrites it as a clean intent, and

the owner's answer and the rewrite (cheaper to read, and analyzable). (2) The
owner had assumed translating Korean to English would cost more tokens and was
surprised it costs less. So: owner answers in Korean, everything else (the
assistant's text and the contextual rewrite beside each owner answer) in

to save; but if equal, the owner's own words should not be rewritten into
English, because e.g. `ㅇ` would become "yes"/"ok" and a typo like `ㅇㄴ` (ㄴ is

Assistant adopted both points as better than its own proposal:

## § Input 7 — what does a trigger actually do?

> 트리거 라는 건 기록할 대화를 체크하는 거지? 체크 해놓은 대화들을 스펙 확정 등 승인한 단위 당 저장하는 거고? 그러니까 대화 기록 시점이 스킬 수정하거나 하면 기록 시작 되고 수정 승인 되면 저장 되고? 이 부분을 좀 더 자세한 설명 듣고싶어

Owner's model: a trigger marks conversations to record; marked conversations
are saved per approved unit; recording starts when a skill is modified and is

Assistant corrected the model: a skill is instructions, not a program; nothing
runs in the background or records. A trigger is a condition under which the

from whatever is in the assistant's context at commit time. This exposes two
risks that became rules: (a) the raw exchange dies with the session, so the
entry must be written in the session where the decisions happened; (b) long
sessions compact their early turns, so a long unit needs an early draft.
Noted, without acting on it, that `question-gate`'s pending-decision ledger
could feed the blackbox later.
